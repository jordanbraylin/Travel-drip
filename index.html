TravelDrip-owned materials

All TravelDrip names, logos, interface layouts, product text, graphics, software, workflows, and original site materials are owned by TravelDrip or its licensors unless marked otherwise. Users may not copy, resell, modify, scrape, or redistribute these materials without written permission.

User content

Users retain ownership of photos, videos, documents, captions, reviews, receipts, and trip content they upload. By adding content, users give TravelDrip permission to host, process, resize, display, share with selected trip members, and sync that content as needed to provide the service.

Prohibited content

Users may not upload content they do not own or have permission to use, including copyrighted photos, music, videos, documents, logos, maps, tickets, or vendor materials. Users are responsible for confirming they have the right to share content inside a trip.

Copyright complaints

Copyright owners can request review by providing the copyrighted work, the TravelDrip content location, contact information, a good-faith statement, and a statement that the report is accurate. Verified infringing content may be removed or restricted.

1. ReportSubmit the content location, ownership details, and why the use is unauthorized.
2. ReviewTravelDrip reviews the request, preserves relevant audit information, and may temporarily restrict disputed content.
3. ActionContent may be removed, restored, or escalated if the uploader provides valid permission or a counter-notice.
4. Repeat misuseAccounts that repeatedly upload infringing content may lose upload access or be removed from TravelDrip.

This mock policy is product copy for the TravelDrip prototype and should be reviewed by a qualified attorney before production launch.
