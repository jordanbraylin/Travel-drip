User content

Users retain ownership of photos, videos, documents, captions, reviews, receipts, and trip content they upload. By adding content, users give Travel-Drip permission to host, process, resize, display, share with selected trip members, and sync that content as needed to provide the service.

Prohibited content

Users may not upload content they do not own or have permission to use, including copyrighted photos, music, videos, documents, logos, maps, tickets, or vendor materials. Users are responsible for confirming they have the right to share content inside a trip.

Copyright complaints

Copyright owners can request review by providing the copyrighted work, the Travel-Drip content location, contact information, a good-faith statement, and a statement that the report is accurate. Verified infringing content may be removed or restricted.

Install Travel-Drip

Add Travel-Drip to your Home Screen

  1. Open Travel-Drip in Safari on iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Share icon in the browser toolbar.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add and launch Travel-Drip like an app.

Android Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and desktop Chrome can show an Install Travel-Drip prompt automatically when available.

Secure confirmation

Confirm wallet payment

Enter your 4-digit trip wallet PIN to add funds.

For this mock, any 4-digit PIN confirms the payment. In production, PIN checks must happen on the server and card data must never touch this app.

Secure corporate access

Enter Corporate Event Code

Enter the secure code provided by your company to access this corporate retreat or event. Corporate details remain hidden until verification succeeds.

Access is matched to the correct company event, employee record, role, active code, and unexpired session. Failed attempts never reveal whether an employee, event, or company exists.

No attendee names, schedules, hotels, documents, budgets, cards, messages, or approved photos are shown before verification.
