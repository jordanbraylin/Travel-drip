## Campaign Platform

**Campaign name:** Get the Trip Out of the Group Chat

**Brand promise:** Plan together. Travel better.

**Campaign headline:** Your next trip. One shared place.

**Primary call to action:** Install Travel-Drip Free

**Supporting message:** Plan the itinerary, coordinate transportation, manage shared money, keep everyone updated, and save the memories without chasing messages and screenshots.

## Campaign Goal

Turn interested travelers into active Travel-Drip groups. A successful conversion is not only an install. It is a new account that completes these activation steps:

1. Creates a trip or event.
2. Invites at least two people.
3. Adds a booking, activity, poll, or wallet goal.
4. Returns within seven days.

## Priority Audiences

### The Group Trip Starter

The friend or family member who creates the group chat, compares options, collects money, and reminds everyone what is happening next.

**Message:** Stop planning the whole trip across five different apps.

### The Celebration Planner

People organizing birthdays, destination weddings, reunions, bachelor or bachelorette trips, graduation trips, and milestone events.

**Message:** Give every guest one place for invitations, RSVPs, schedules, travel details, and updates.

### The Frequent Traveler

Solo travelers, couples, and small groups who need bookings, boarding passes, transportation, weather, spending, and memories organized together.

**Message:** Everything your trip needs, ready when you need it.

### The Retreat Organizer

Corporate and organization planners coordinating approved travel, attendees, schedules, transportation, documents, and communications.

**Message:** Keep the experience polished for travelers and controlled for organizers.

## Creative Direction

- Show the actual product experience prominently.
- Lead with a recognizable planning problem, then reveal the shared dashboard.
- Use bright travel photography, Caribbean teal, aqua, coral, sunshine yellow, and deep navy text.
- Keep layouts clean, social, and energetic rather than corporate.
- Use compact interface cards with horizontal text and realistic mobile proportions.
- Feature real moments: choosing a destination, splitting a dinner bill, checking a flight, coordinating a ride, and sharing photos.

## Referral Loop

**Program name:** Bring the Crew

Every new trip creator receives a personal invite link after creating a trip. The progress message should read:

**Your trip gets better together. Invite two travelers to unlock the full group dashboard.**

Suggested beta reward after two accepted invitations:

- Founding Traveler profile badge.
- Early access to AI itinerary tools.
- Entry into a monthly travel credit drawing after legal review.

## First-Run Activation

Keep the post-install experience to one clear checklist:

- Name your trip.
- Add the destination and dates.
- Invite your people.
- Add your first plan.

Celebrate completion with:

**Your Travel-Drip is ready. Invite the group and start planning together.**

## Four-Week Rollout

### Week 1: Tease the Problem

- Post group-chat and screenshot-chaos creative.
- Recruit a small set of travel and event-planning creators.
- Open a beta waitlist and collect trip type, destination, and group size.

### Week 2: Launch the Product

- Publish the hero visual and short-form video.
- Send the launch email.
- Run ads focused on group-trip planners and celebration organizers.
- Route every CTA to the install page with campaign tracking.

### Week 3: Prove the Use Cases

- Release transportation, shared wallet, itinerary, and memories demonstrations.
- Share beta-user trip setups and creator walkthroughs with permission.
- Retarget visitors who viewed the install page but did not create a trip.

### Week 4: Activate the Group

- Launch Bring the Crew referrals.
- Send incomplete-setup reminders.
- Highlight trips that invited members and added their first plan.
- Interview activated groups and turn objections into the next creative set.

## Measurement Plan

Track the complete funnel:

- Ad click-through rate.
- Landing-page-to-install rate.
- Install-to-account rate.
- Account-to-first-trip rate.
- Percentage inviting at least two people.
- Percentage adding a first booking, activity, poll, or wallet goal.
- Day 1 and Day 7 return rate.
- Cost per activated trip.
- Invites accepted per active trip.

Initial beta targets should be treated as hypotheses and revised after the first 100 qualified visitors and first 25 activated trips.

## Campaign Tracking

Use consistent campaign parameters:

```text
utm_source={channel}
utm_medium={paid-social|creator|email|referral|organic}
utm_campaign=get-the-trip-out-of-the-group-chat
utm_content={creative-name}
```

Record the originating campaign on account creation and retain it through first-trip activation.

## Launch Guardrails

- Do not advertise live provider features until their production connections have been verified.
- Describe wallet and payment capabilities according to the active PCI-compliant provider implementation.
- Use real beta-user stories only with written permission.
- Link privacy, terms, support, and data-deletion information near account creation.
- Make “free” claims precise if paid plans or transaction fees are introduced.
- Keep the campaign destination focused on one action: create the first trip.
