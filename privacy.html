Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Travel-Drip helps people plan trips, coordinate events, store travel details, and preserve memories. This summary explains the information we collect and how we protect it.

Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, we may store your name, username, email address, profile details, trip content, trusted contacts, travel documents, messages, media, and preferences.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, provide trip features, send requested notifications, protect the service, and keep content available to the people you authorize.

Storage and protection

Account credentials are handled by Supabase Auth. Application records use authenticated access and database row-level security. Private files should be stored in private object storage and accessed through authorized links. We do not place service-role or other server secrets in the browser.

Sharing and choices

You control trip, profile, media, and contact visibility through the app. We do not automatically publish photos or videos without your explicit sharing action. You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your account data.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Travel-Drip support team through the support channel provided with your beta invitation.
